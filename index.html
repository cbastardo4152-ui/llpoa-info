Welcome! Lake Latonka is a private community. This page offers an easy-to-read introduction. The official documents linked below contain the full requirements and govern if a summary is incomplete. Guests should check with their hosting property owner before using community amenities.

👋 Visiting Lake Latonka? Start here.

Come with your host

Property owners and their immediate families may use community areas. Other friends and relatives must be accompanied by the property owner. Know your host's lot number and subdivision name.

Drive slowly

The speed limit on Lake Latonka roads is 15 mph. The dam is a restricted area: no pedestrian, vehicle or fishing access.

Respect the community

Keep pets leashed off their owner's property. Clean up after your group. Contact the association before bringing a large group to the beach or park.

Check before boating

Lake access is private. Watercraft must belong to property owners and display the required current LLPOA stickers. Do not bring an outside boat or personal watercraft.

Source: 2022 Lake Rules, General Rules 1–5 and Lake Rules 2–3; 2024 Restrictions §§10–11, 15.

🚤 Lake & boating

Wake hours

11 a.m.–7 p.m. Maximum 35 mph except designated no-wake areas and within 75 feet of shore.

No-wake hours

7:01 p.m.–10:59 a.m. Motorized boats must travel no faster than 5 mph, aiming to create no wake.

Direction of travel

Motorized boats travel counterclockwise, with left turns. Cross the lake using a direct perpendicular path when necessary.

Safety essentials

No jet skis or wave runners. Towed watersports require a qualified driver and an observer age 12 or older. Skiers, wakeboarders and tubers must wear Coast Guard-approved life jackets; children under 13 must wear a life jacket on moving motorized boats.

Owners must register motorized boats and display current stickers. Nonmotorized craft also need current LLPOA stickers. Read the full restrictions for ownership limits, ice rules and other boating requirements.

Source: 2024 Restrictions §§11.02–11.19 and 2022 Lake Rules.

🏖️ Beach & park

At the beach

  • No animals, alcohol or glass containers.
  • Swim only inside designated buoy areas; no lifeguards are on duty.
  • No fishing in the beach area.
  • No fires except in designated grills.
  • Remove all litter and supervise children and guests.

At the park

  • Keep restrooms clean and remove your litter.
  • Supervise children using playground equipment.
  • Use designated grills only; no vehicles inside the park.
  • Check current park closing time with the association before visiting in the evening.

Document discrepancy awaiting board confirmation: The 2022 Lake Rules say the park closes at 10 p.m.; the 2024 recorded Restrictions say sunset. The 2024 Restrictions also specify beach closure at sunset unless there is an association-sponsored event. Confirm the current applicable hours with LLPOA.

Source: 2022 Lake Rules, Beach and Park Rules; 2024 Restrictions §§13–14.

🏡 Information for residents

Construction & improvements

Submit plans for homes, docks, garages, fences and other covered improvements to the Building Control Committee and obtain approval before starting. Consult the recorded Restrictions for requirements.

Membership & governance

The association has a five-member board. Its bylaws describe membership, elections, meetings, committees, records and association governance.

Rules & enforcement

The March 2025 fines schedule lists $250 Type 1, $100 Type 2 and $25 Type 3 violations. It encourages education and private resolution before fines where appropriate. The Restrictions contain the full enforcement and appeal procedures.

Sources: 2024 Bylaws Arts. II–VII; 2024 Restrictions §§2, 12, 16; 2025 Fines Addendum.
